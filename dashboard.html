Why This Data Matters
How It Works

Passive Facial Telemetry

Emotica reads the player's face every 2 seconds using on-device facial landmark detection. No camera data ever leaves the browser. Emotion states are classified locally and logged with gameplay events.

What's Unique

Emotion × Difficulty Correlation

Traditional analytics tells you where players quit. Emotica tells you what they were feeling 10 seconds before they quit — and shows whether the adaptive system's intervention kept them playing.

Business Value

The Retention Equation

High frustration + no intervention = churn. Emotica quantifies both the problem and the fix. The Retention Score (Engagement ÷ Frustration) gives your design team one number to optimize.

Privacy First

Consent-Based Collection

Players choose whether to share data before their first game. No camera footage, no PII — only timestamped mood states and game events. Fully anonymizable for aggregate analysis at scale.
